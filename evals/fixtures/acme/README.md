# Acme fixture (illustrative)

Acme is an invented company: invoicing software for trades businesses, Series C, about 400 people, 12 squads,
with an AI assistant that drafts quotes and chases invoices. Every account, name, email, phone number, card
number, address and ARR figure here is invented and illustrative. The card numbers are published test numbers.

The files are shaped like each system's documented export: Zendesk ticket pages with sideloaded users,
Intercom conversation search results, and CSV exports of call notes and NPS answers. They carry, on purpose:
a ticket repeated across two pages, a complaint sent by ticket and by chat, personal data of every kind the
redactor handles, an NPS score with no comment, and a ticket that tries to give the model instructions.
